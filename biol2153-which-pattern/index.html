Case files: dominance patterns

  1. Enter your full name. You get your own data set, so your numbers won’t match your classmates’.

  3. You get 3 checks per case. Correct on the 1st check earns full points, on the 2nd 75%, on the 3rd 50%. After 3 checks the answers are shown.
  4. Written answers must be typed in your own words. Pasting is turned off.
  5. Finish the exit ticket, then copy your report into the Moodle recitation assignment.

Your progress is saved in this browser. Type your name the same way to come back to it.
